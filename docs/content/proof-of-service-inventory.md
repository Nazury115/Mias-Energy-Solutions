## Publication readiness

- Ask for original camera images where available. These compressed files may work for cards/gallery but should not be stretched into large high-resolution desktop hero photos.
- Obtain permission for photographs, identifiable people and client premises; separately record permission for client names/logos and testimonial attribution.
- Identify work-in-progress views honestly. Open enclosures, tools, lifting operations and workers do not establish a commissioned installation or a defect by themselves.
- Verify customer names, project dates, equipment capacities and outcomes before captions are written. Do not derive them from filename timestamps or visual guesses.
- Preserve originals; prepare optimized publication copies only after approval. Check for private labels/location metadata and select crops that do not misrepresent the work.

First useful content delivery: three labelled case studies spanning water, electrical/control and solar if suitable examples exist. A smaller verified selection is sufficient while the full customer list develops. Use the [project record template](business-intake.md#project-record-template).
