# Deferred suggestion: solar planner

On 6 November 2026 Henry requested removal of the solar planner, citing the ongoing changes in equipment prices and a preference to improve the existing system. It is outside the active roadmap and is not a launch requirement.

The normal solar service inquiry remains available. Customers describe their requirements; staff assess them and prepare a quotation using current equipment and prices.

If reconsidered later, start with a fresh specification and approval. Confirm a named technical reviewer, supported products, a reliable process for maintaining prices, clear quotation validity and independent worked examples. No automatic sizing or price recommendation is currently promised.

The retired [011 specification](../../specs/011-solar-planner.md), plan and test record remain historical references. Existing appliance details already saved with a request are retained privately and read-only; new planner submissions are unavailable.
