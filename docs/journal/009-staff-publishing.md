# Trying increment 009

1. Sign in as Demo Staff and open **Publishing** in the workspace. Create a project story or testimonial with **Fictional test content** checked for local testing.
2. Fill the draft. For a project, choose its service family and province, then choose an existing work photo or register a JPEG/PNG. A registered upload must also be selected in the draft. Save.
3. Open **Preview saved draft**. It shows saved text and selected photos. Private permission evidence stays in the editor.
4. Sign in as an Owner in a separate browser profile. Open the same entry under Publishing, review its saved preview, enter an approval reason and confirm the facts and permissions. Select **Publish saved draft**.
5. Use **View published content** or visit Projects. The project page, map links and sitemap use the approved version. Testimonials appear on Projects.
6. Change the draft as Staff and save it. The previous approved version stays public. The Owner can approve the replacement when ready.
7. As Owner, enter an unpublish reason and select **Unpublish**. The public story and uploaded photo links stop resolving.

Photos are limited to 5 MiB each and eight uploads per entry. The private inventory records the description, source and permission evidence. Test records retain their classification and are hidden in launch mode.
