# Trying local quotations and invoices

1. As Owner, open **Documents → Business and bank settings**. Review the private values imported from the corrected business invoice, complete the tax-treatment note, confirm the details and save.
2. As Staff or Owner, open an existing job and use **Quotations and invoices** to create a draft. The global **Documents** page can also select a job.
3. Enter the customer details, agreed payment terms and line items. Line discounts are ZMW amounts; the overall discount is a percentage. Save the draft.
4. Select **Preview draft** to open the saved version. Use **Edit draft** to return to the editor. Save all changes, then select **Submit for review**.
5. As Owner, open **Documents → Awaiting Owner review**. Select **Preview draft** or **Review draft**. The submitted preview appears above **Approve and issue** and **Deny draft**. Approval requires a reason and review confirmation. Denial requires a return reason, visible to Staff.
6. As Staff, open a returned draft, read the feedback, edit, save and submit again. Saving a submitted draft withdraws it from review; an older open Owner page cannot approve that version. Changes to global settings do not change a submitted preview.
7. On a complete saved draft or issued document, choose **PDF** or **Excel** under **Download format**, then **Download document**. The Excel workbook includes typed amounts and calculation formulas. Draft downloads are Staff/Owner only. Linked customers see the same selector for issued documents in their job portal.
8. To correct an issued document, Staff or Owner selects **Create revision draft**. Edit, save and submit it, then obtain Owner approval. The original becomes superseded and retains its original downloads.
9. An Owner can cancel the current issued document with a reason. Customers can see its cancelled status and retain access to the original issued copy while their job access remains active.
